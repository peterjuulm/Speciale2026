Reading notes

Layer. 1 is compiler output, 2 published files and migrators, 3 the frontend export, 4 archives and signing, 5 container images. M rows describe how we measured. A finding that spans two layers is listed under both.

State. Closed or stable means closed or stable in the recorded experiment, and can still carry qualifications. Open means unresolved, and may concern development output that never ships. Rejected means the result cannot support the original claim. The table calls these green, red and rejected.

Evidence. The table is an index. Open a finding for the full observation, fix and recorded run. [V] marks a repeated measurement and [I] an inference. The experiment notes are the evidence. The status board says what runs next.
